# Share a course for review

1. Select **For review**.

2. Enter the names or email addresses of your reviewers in the Add names or emails field.

3. Review the **Who has access** section to confirm only invited people can access the project.

4. Select **Invite to comment** or **Copy link** to share it with your reviewers.
