# Best practices for publishing from Content Composer to ALM

- Write a specific learning objective before generating the outline. The objective controls what the AI prioritizes in your source file, the outline structure, and what the quiz tests.

- Review the outline before generating the course. Changing lesson names through the chat at the outline stage takes seconds; changing them after generation requires returning to outline conversations.

- Enable **Restrict output to content in files** when accuracy to your organization's specific procedures is required. For example, for compliance or policy training.

- Review all generated content, assessments, and learning objectives before publishing. AI output is probabilistic and may vary between generations.

- Use the **Accordion** and **Tab** components to add depth within a topic when the fixed Lessons > Topics hierarchy is not granular enough for your content.
