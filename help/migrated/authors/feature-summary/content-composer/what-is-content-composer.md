# What is Adobe Learning Manager Content Composer

Adobe Learning Manager Content Composer combines every stage of course creation into a single AI-guided workflow. You describe what you want to teach, and the product generates a structured outline and full course content, including images, knowledge checks, and a graded quiz, based on your inputs.

Content Composer brings every stage of learning creation into a single workflow and supports co-creation with AI that understands instructional design, helping you create learning that's not just faster to produce, but more effective by design.

When your course is ready, you can:

- Publish courses directly to Adobe Learning Manager and gain actionable insights into learning effectiveness through comprehensive learner analytics.
- Download it as a SCORM package to use in any compatible LMS.
