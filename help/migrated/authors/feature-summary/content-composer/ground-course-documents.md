# Ground your course in your own documents

Most AI course tools generate content from general knowledge - a problem when your training needs to match your organization's actual policies, procedures, or regulatory language, rather than a plausible-sounding approximation.

Content Composer lets you control exactly where the AI draws from. When you upload source files and select **Restrict output to content in files**, Content Composer generates every part of the course - outline, lessons, and quiz - only from what you provided. The AI doesn't introduce outside information, even to fill a gap it identifies as missing.

This matters most for:

- **Compliance and regulatory training**: where course content must match your actual policy language.

- **Procedure and SOP training**: where a "close enough" AI-generated step could be actively wrong.

- **Product or systems training**: where the course needs to reflect your specific configuration, not a generic version of the tool.

If you'd rather let the AI supplement your material with its own general knowledge - useful for broader or less sensitive topics - leave the restriction unselected. If you have no source material at all, choose **Generate the course without source files**.
