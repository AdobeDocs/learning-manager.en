# Who Adobe Learning Manager Content Composer is for

Adobe Learning Manager Content Composer is designed for anyone who needs to create a structured course without starting from scratch.

| **Role**              | **How Content Composer helps**                                                                                                                                                                                     |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Author**            | Creates structured, effective courses. Whether converting your own knowledge into a course, working from existing documents and source material, or refining AI-generated content to meet specific learning goals. |
| **L&D Administrator** | Provisions access, sets organization themes, and publishes completed courses to Adobe Learning Manager.                                                                                                            |
| **L&D Manager**       | Reviews and approves courses before they are distributed to learners.                                                                                                                                              |
