# Before you start

Before launching Adobe Learning Manager Content Composer, confirm the following:

>[!IMPORTANT]
>
>You have access to Content Composer through your Adobe Learning Manager subscription. Contact your administrator if you're not sure whether Content Composer is included in your organization's plan.

* You have a general idea of the subject area you want to cover. You do not need full clarity before you begin.

* The AI guides you through defining the course title, audience, and learning objective through conversation.

* Source documents are optional. You can proceed without uploading any files, and the AI will generate content based on your input.

* If you have existing material, you want the course to draw from, you can upload it during the Brief stage. Supported file formats are PDF, Markdown (.md), PowerPoint (.pptx), and others, each up to 100 MB.
