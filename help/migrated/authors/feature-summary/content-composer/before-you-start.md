# Before you start

Before launching Adobe Learning Manager Content Composer, confirm the following:

>[!IMPORTANT]
>
>You must sign in with a valid Adobe Creative Cloud account. If you don't already have one, you can create a free account through Adobe Express. For more information, view [Create a free Adobe Express account](https://helpx.adobe.com/express/web/adobe-express-subscription/free.html). After creating your Adobe credentials, launch Content Composer and sign in to start creating courses. If your organization already has a Creative Cloud subscription, contact your administrator to provision a Creative Cloud account for you before signing in to Content Composer.

For the best experience with Content Composer, **Google Chrome is the recommended browser**. Firefox and Safari may show differences in functionality or behavior.

## Optional requirements

* You have a general idea of the subject area you want to cover. You do not need full clarity before you begin.

* The AI guides you through defining the course title, audience, and learning objective through conversation.

* Source documents are optional. You can proceed without uploading any files, and the AI will generate content based on your input.

* If you have existing material, you want the course to draw from, you can upload it during the Brief stage. Supported file formats are PDF, Markdown (.md), PowerPoint (.pptx), and others, each up to 100 MB.
