# About raise hand and reactions

Instructors and participants can use the raise hand and reactions features to interact during a Live Hub session. Learners raise their hands to signal that they want to speak, and send reactions to give quick, non-verbal feedback. Instructors can view reactions and raised hands in real time to manage participation, all without interrupting the session flow.

## Key benefits

The raise hand and reactions features provide the following benefits:

* **Encourage participation**: Let learners signal that they want to speak or ask a question without interrupting the session.

* **Gather quick feedback**: Enable learners to respond with reactions such as thumbs up, applause, or thinking, giving Instructors an instant sense of the room.

* **Manage interactions**: Allow Instructors to lower an individual learner's hand or clear all raised hands to keep discussions organized.

* **Stay non-disruptive**: Support engagement through visual cues and reactions that appear in real time without pausing the session flow.

## Roles and permissions

Both Instructors and Learners can raise their hand and send reactions in a Live Hub session, but the available actions depend on the user's role.
