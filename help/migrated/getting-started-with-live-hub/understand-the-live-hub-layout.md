# Understand the Live Hub layout

In Adobe Learning Manager Live Hub, the session room is designed to help Instructors and Learners collaborate effectively during live sessions. The layout includes multiple panels and controls that allow you to manage audio, video, participants, and interactions in real time.

This article provides an overview of the Live Hub interface and its layout. It explains the different components of the room, such as the control panel, Attendees panel, and other key elements, with labeled references to help you navigate the interface.

## Key components of the classroom layout

The Live Hub layout is organized into panels and toolbars that help Instructors and Learners interact, collaborate, and manage the session effectively.

### Control bar (bottom toolbar)

The Control bar provides quick access to the following in-session controls and settings.

* **Microphone controls**: Turn your microphone on or off during the session.

* **Video controls**: Enable or disable your camera.

* **Reactions**: Use emojis and quick responses to interact during the session.

* **Raise hand**: Indicate that you want to ask a question or speak.

* **Share screen**: Present your screen during the session (based on permissions).

* **More actions**: Access the following additional tools and settings:

  * **Captions**: Turn live captions on or off to view spoken content as text during the session.

  * **Whiteboard**: Open the whiteboard to collaborate visually by drawing, writing, or annotating with other participants.

  * **Room settings**: Configure session settings, including **Participant permissions**, **Recording**, **AI**, and **Privacy** options to control participant access, recording preferences, AI features, and participant visibility.

### Polls & Quizzes panel

The Polls & Quizzes panel provides an overview of participant involvement during the session. You can:

* Monitor learner engagement levels.

* View participation indicators.

* Access engagement insights.

### Attendees panel

The Attendees panel lists all participants in the session. You can:

* View enrolled attendees, including present and absent participants.

* Identify participant roles, such as Instructor or Learner.

* Search for participants.

* Monitor attendance in real time.

### Chat panel

The Chat panel enables real-time communication between participants. You can:

* Send and receive messages during the session.

* Ask questions or respond to discussions.

* Interact with Instructors and other learners.

### Breakouts panel

The Breakouts panel lets Instructors divide participants into smaller groups for focused discussions and collaborative activities. You can:

* Create and manage breakout rooms during the session.

* Assign participants to groups automatically or manually.

* Move between rooms to monitor and support each group.

* Bring all participants back to the main session when the activity ends.

### Miro app

Open **More apps** to access integrated tools that extend collaboration during the session. From here, you can launch the **Miro** app to work together on a shared visual board in real time. With the Miro board, you can:

* Brainstorm ideas together on a shared, infinite canvas.

* Add sticky notes, shapes, and diagrams as the session progresses.

* Collaborate visually with all participants without leaving the session.

### Participant engagement

Participant engagement helps track participants' activity and involvement in real time during the session. You can:

* View overall learner engagement levels.

* Monitor participation based on interactions such as responses, reactions, and activity.

* Identify whether learners are actively engaged or need attention.
