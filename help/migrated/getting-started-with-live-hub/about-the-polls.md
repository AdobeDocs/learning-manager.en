# About polls in Live Hub

The Poll feature allows Instructors to create and launch polls during a live session to gather responses from learners in real time. You can build polls manually by choosing a question type such as multiple choice, multiple answer, or short answer, or generate polls quickly using AI-assisted options like the Icebreaker poll and the Knowledge check poll.

Learners can respond to active polls, update their answers, and view shared results. All poll activities are managed from the **Polls & Quizzes** panel.

## Key benefits

* **Increase learner engagement**: Encourage active participation by allowing learners to respond to questions during the session beyond listening.

* **Check understanding in real time**: Assess how well learners understand a topic and identify areas that may need further explanation.

* **Start sessions with icebreakers**: Use polls at the beginning of a session to warm up participants, encourage early interaction, and create a more engaging learning environment.

* **Gather instant feedback**: Collect opinions, preferences, or feedback from learners in real time while continuing the session, without pausing ongoing instruction or content sharing.

## Who can access the polls

The **Poll** feature is available to both Instructors and Learners in a Live Hub session. Available actions vary based on the user role.
