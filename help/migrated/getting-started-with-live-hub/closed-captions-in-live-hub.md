Closed captions transcribe spoken content in real time during a Live Hub session. Participants see the spoken text on screen as the conversation happens. Captions are useful when audio is unclear, for example, in noisy environments or when participants prefer to read along. Closed captions are especially useful in situations where audio is unclear, such as in noisy environments or when participants prefer to read along with the discussion.

## How do closed captions work

In a Live Hub session, closed captions are generated automatically. Captions display in a dedicated panel and update continuously as participants speak. Both Instructors and Learners can view captions.

Each participant can show or hide captions as they prefer. Changing this setting does not affect other participants.

The **closed captions** feature is available to both Instructors and Learners in a Live Hub session. Available actions vary based on the user role.
