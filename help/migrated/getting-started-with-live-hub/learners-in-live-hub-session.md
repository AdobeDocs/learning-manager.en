# Participate as a Learner in a Live Hub session

As a Learner in Adobe Learning Manager, you join Live Hub sessions to attend live, Instructor-led training. Your role is to join the scheduled session, take part using the collaboration tools in the session room, and review the recording afterward.

## Join the session

Open your enrolled course and join the Live Hub session at the scheduled date and time:

1.  Sign in to Adobe Learning Manager and go to **My Learnings**.

1.  Select the enrolled course.

1.  Select **Start** > **Go to Session**, then select **Join**.

## Take part in the session

During the session, you collaborate with the Instructor and other learners using the session tools. You can:

* Chat with everyone in the Everyone tab, or message the Instructor and other learners in the Private tab.

* Ask questions for the Instructor to respond to.

* Raise your hand and send reactions.

* Respond to polls and quizzes launched by the Instructor.

* Join breakout rooms for group activities and select **Ask for Help** when you need the Instructor.

* Share and annotate your screen when the Instructor lets participants present.

## After the session

Once the session ends, open the course page to review the session recording and any files shared during the session.
