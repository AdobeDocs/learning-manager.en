# About the Attendees panel in Live Hub

The Attendees panel in a Live Hub allows Instructors to view learners and manage their interactions during a session. It provides a centralized view of all attendees and helps Instructors monitor participation and engagement in real time.

The panel displays attendee names, roles, and interaction status, making it easy to identify who is present in the session and who has not yet joined. Instructors can use the panel to manage participant permissions, monitor engagement, and control learner interactions such as microphone access, reactions, and raised hands.

## Key benefits

* **Improve session visibility**: Displays all attendees in one place, making it easier to see who has joined.

* **Manage participation easily**: Provides quick access to attendee controls that help Instructors manage learner interactions during the session.

* **Support learner interaction**: Enables engagement through features such as reactions, raised hands, and private messaging.

* **Track attendance**: Shows learners who are currently present and those enrolled but have not yet joined the session.

## Who can use the Attendees panel

The Attendees panel is available to both Instructors and Learners in a Live Hub session. Available actions vary based on the user role.
