# Use reactions and raised hands

As a Learner, use reactions and raised hands to participate in a session without interrupting the presenter. Both options are available from the session control bar and help you communicate with Instructors and other
participants in real time.

## Raise your hand in the session

The **Raise hand** option in the control bar allows the participants to signal that they want to speak or ask a question during a session. To raise your hand, select ![raise-hand](./assets/raise-hand.svg) from the control bar. Your raised hand is visible to the Instructor and appears in the **Attendees** list.

If you want to lower your hand, select again from the session controls. The raised-hand indicator is removed from the attendees list.

## Send reactions

Reactions let you provide quick, non-verbal feedback during a session.

To send a reaction:

1. Select ![send-reactions](./assets/send-reactions.svg) from the control bar.

   ![Send Reactions Control Bar](assets/send-reactions-control-bar.png)
   *Select the Reactions from the control bar.*

1. Select one of the following reactions:

   1. Thumbs up
   1. Heart
   1. Applause
   1. Laughter
   1. Surprise
   1. Thinking

   The selected reaction is displayed to all attendees in the session.
