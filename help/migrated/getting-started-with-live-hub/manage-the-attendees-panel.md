# Manage the Attendees panel

Use the **Attendees** panel to view and manage participants in a Live Hub session. The panel displays a real-time list of attendees, including learners who have joined the session and those enrolled but have not yet joined. It also shows each attendee's role and interaction status.

As an Instructor, you can use the Attendees panel to manage learner participation, configure attendee permissions, and moderate interactions during the session. You can perform actions such as muting microphones, lowering raised hands, sending private messages, and removing learners from the session.

This article explains how to access the Attendees panel and manage learners during a Live Hub session.

## View the Attendees panel

The **Attendees** panel shows everyone in the session. To open it, select the **Attendees** icon in the control bar. The panel lists all learners and updates automatically as participants join or leave.

![Attendees Panel Overview](assets/attendees-panel-overview.png)
*Live Hub interface showing the Attendees panel.*

The following options are available in the panel:

| **Option** | **Description** |
|----|----|
| **Search** | Type in the **Search** field to find a participant. The list filters as you type. |
| **Present** | Shows learners currently in the session, along with their roles (e.g., Instructor) and microphone status. The number in parentheses indicates how many are present. |
| **Not joined** | Shows learners enrolled in the session who haven't joined yet. Select the arrow to expand the list. |

## Attendees panel settings

To manage attendee preferences:

1.  Select **More options (⋯)** in the **Attendees** panel.

1.  You can take the following available actions:

    1.  **Lower all hands**: Lower all raised hands for participants.

    1.  **Mute all participants**: Mute the microphones of all participants.

    1.  **Turn off camera for all participants**: Turn off the cameras of all participants.

## Manage learners during the session

The **Attendees** panel lets you manage individual learners during the session. You can moderate interactions and take quick actions for a specific learner when needed, such as muting their microphone, lowering a raised hand, sending a private message, or viewing their profile, helping you maintain an organized learning environment.

To update learner permissions:

1.  Navigate to the learner in the **Attendees** panel.

1.  Select the **More options (⋯)** icon next to the learner's name.

    ![Learner More Options Menu](assets/learner-more-options-menu.png)
    *More options menu showing the learner's actions from the Attendees panel.*

1.  Select one of the following actions:

| **Action** | **Description** |
|----|----|
| **Mute** | Turns off the learner's microphone. |
| **Lower hand** | Removes the learner's raised hand indicator. |
| **Send a private message** | Opens a private chat with the learner. |
| **Remove from session** | Removes the learner from the session. |
| **View profile** | Displays the learner's profile information. |
