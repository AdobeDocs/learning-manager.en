# New experience league announcements

## New customer communication 

We are excited to announce that in our journey to be better connected to the Adobe family of solutions, Adobe Learning Manager will be adding one more channel to the support intake process. From May 12th, 2025, you will now be able to create cases directly from Adobe Experience League. Experience League is currently home to Adobe Learning Manager self-help documentation, user guides and best practices, and will become your primary resource for all support needs from self-service to agent-driven communication. We appreciate your understanding and support as we make this important transition.

## Adobe Learning Manager Content Composer Help

**From concept to course in minutes**

Most tools generate content. Content Composer generates learning. A conversational AI guides authors through training goals, source material, and learning objectives, then produces instructionally sound courses, assessments, and media that are editable, on-brand, and ready to publish.

Create structured, publish-ready courses from a plain-language prompt, no prior authoring experience required. Start with the overview, follow the end-to-end tutorial, or jump directly to the topic you need.

**Key highlights**

- The AI asks guided questions to turn training goals into clear, measurable learning objectives; no instructional design experience required.
- Authors upload existing documents, policies, or decks to ground the AI in your organisation's specific content. The AI generates a brief and outline; authors accept or edit before anything is built.
- Courses, assessments, and media are generated using structured learning principles — keeping output educationally sound, not just fast.
- Finished courses publish directly to Adobe Learning Manager. No separate tool, no manual SCORM export.
- Creation, management, and reporting stay on one system, reducing the operational overhead of managing multiple authoring and delivery platforms.
