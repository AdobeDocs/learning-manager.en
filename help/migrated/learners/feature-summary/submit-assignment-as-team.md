# Submit an assignment as a team

View your team, work together on the assignment, upload one file for the whole team, and check your team's evaluation result.

When you are assigned to a team, you receive a notification. Launch it from the learner app.

![](assets/team-submission30.png)

In a team-based file submission module, any team member can upload the file for everyone. Your instructor evaluates the work once, and the outcome applies to the whole team. To learn how the feature works, see [how team submissions work](Team_submissions_and_rubrics_in_Adobe_Learning_Manager.md#how-team-submissions-work).

## View your team and the evaluation plan

1. Open the course in the Learner app, and then go to the course overview page. Team activities are marked as team activities in the course outline.

   ![](assets/team-submission31.png)

1. Select **View team members** to view a list of other learners who are in the same team as you.

   ![](assets/team-submission32.png)

1. If you aren't on a team yet, a message tells you that the activity starts after you're assigned to a team. Contact your instructor.
1. If the author allows it, view what you will be assessed on. You can read the assessment description and each criterion's description. You can't view the answer options, points, or weightage.

You receive a notification and an email when you're added to a team, removed from a team, or when a team member is added or removed.

## Submit the team's file

1. On the course overview page, open the team-based activity.
1. Agree with your team on the file to submit. Select **Upload**.

   ![](assets/team-submission33.png)

1. Upload the file. The status changes to **Evaluation pending**. You can, however, review your submission and submit a different file.

   ![](assets/team-submission34.png)

Your instructor is notified. Every team member can view the status and the submitted file, and download it. All team members receive a notification and an email when any member submits or resubmits.

To replace the file before it's approved, select **View**/**Change Submission**, and then upload a new file.

>[!NOTE]
>
>If the author doesn't require reviewer approval, the activity completes for every team member as soon as the file is submitted.

## View the result

1. Open the team-based activity after your instructor evaluates it. All team members receive a notification.
1. View the status, such as approved or rejected.
1. Read the instructor's comments, if the instructor chose to show them.
1. If the activity has an evaluation plan, view the reviewer's name, the selected option for each question, the written responses, and any remarks. Points for individual options aren't shown.
1. If scoring is on, view your team's overall score.

If the activity doesn't require approval, you see that it's evaluated, with the result.

## Resubmit after a rejection

If your team was rejected, read the comments, and then upload a new file. Your new file replaces the earlier one. Learn what the instructor does in [Evaluate and unsubmit team submissions](Evaluate_and_unsubmit_team_submissions.md).

## Troubleshooting

**The activity says you must be assigned to a team.** You aren't on a team yet. Contact your instructor.

**You can't switch to another instance.** You can't switch instances after your team submits. Switching is allowed only while your team is unlocked.

**The submission disappeared and the status says pending submission.** Your instructor unsubmitted it, and you received a notification and an email. Upload the file again. You don't see the earlier submission or comments.
