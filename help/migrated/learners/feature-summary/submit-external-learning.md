# Submit external learning as a learner

In Adobe Learning Manager, you can log training you complete outside the platform — such as a workshop, certification exam, seminar, or online course, using the **External Learning** feature. Each submission goes to your manager for review. Once approved, the activity appears in your Learner Transcript.

## How the external learning submission process works

External learning submissions follow a three-step process:

1. You fill out a submission form with details about the training you completed and optionally upload proof.

2. Your manager receives an in-platform notification and reviews your submission.

3. Your manager approves or rejects the request. You receive an in-platform notification of the decision.

Approved submissions appear in your **Learner Transcript** as completed external learning activities.

You can submit as many external learning requests as you need. There is no limit on the number of submissions you can create.

**Finding External Learning in the navigation**

External learning is available from **My Learning** in the main navigation. Select **External Learning** to view your submission history and create new submissions.

![](assets/find-external-learning.png)

If you do not see the **External Learning** option, your administrator may not have enabled the feature for your account. Contact your administrator for assistance.

### Submit an external learning request

1. In the left navigation, select **My Learning**.

2. Select the **External Learning** option.

3. Select **Add External Learning**.
![](assets/submit-external-learning-request.png)


4. Fill in the submission form:

    1. **Title:** Enter the name of the training. This field is required.

    2. **Description/notes:** Add any details that help your manager understand the training, such as the provider's name or the learning objectives.

    3. **Start date:** Select the date the training began.

    4. **End date:** Select the date the training was completed.

    5. **Duration:** Enter the total time (in hours) you spent on the training.

    6. **Score:** Enter your score if the training includes an assessment.

    7. **Attachments:** Upload a certificate, transcript, or other document as evidence. Supported file types are PDF, DOC, DOCX, PNG, JPEG, and JPG. The maximum file size is 50 MB.
    ![](assets/add-external-learning.png)

    8. Fill in any additional custom fields your administrator has configured.

5. Select **Submit**.

Your manager receives an in-app notification that a new external learning request is awaiting their review. Your submission appears in your **External Learning** list with the status **Awaiting review**.
![](assets/submission-external-learning-list.png)

>[!NOTE]
>
>You receive an in-platform notification when your manager approves or rejects your submission.

### Edit a pending submission

You can edit a submission while its status is **Awaiting Approval**. Once your manager approves or rejects it, you can no longer edit that submission.

1. In the **External Learning** tab, find the submission you want to update.

2. Select the submission to open it.

3. Select **Edit**.

4. Update any fields as needed. The form shows the latest field configuration set by your administrator, which may include fields that were not present when you originally submitted.

5. Select **Submit**.

Your manager receives a new notification and reviews your updated submission.

**If your submission was rejected:** You cannot edit a rejected submission. To resubmit, create a new external learning request and reference any feedback your manager provided in their review comment.

### External learning submission statuses

| **Status**      | **What it means**                                                                | **Can you edit?**                                       |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Awaiting review | Your submission is pending manager review.                                       | Yes. Select **Edit** from the submission detail page.   |
| Approved        | Your manager approved the submission; it is included in your Learner Transcript. | No.                                                     |
| Rejected        | Your manager rejected the submission; review their comment for guidance.         | No. Create a new external learning request to resubmit. |
