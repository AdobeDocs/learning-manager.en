# Connect your AI client to Adobe Learning Manager

Your integration administrator must set up the connector first. If you don't see an Adobe Learning Manager connector, contact your administrator. **The client is available only to the Adobe Learning Manager Ultimate tier**.

Administrators can refer to [Add Adobe Learning Manager to your AI client.](/help/migrated/administrators/feature-summary/add-alm-to-ai-client.md)

1. Open your organization's AI client, such as Claude or ChatGPT Enterprise.
2. Find the Adobe Learning Manager connector in the list of connectors.
3. Select **Connect**.
4. Sign in with your enterprise identity when prompted.
5. Ask a question about your learning. For ideas, view **sample prompts for learners**.

>[!NOTE]
>
>You sign in once. Your requests run with your own permissions, so you see only what you can access in Adobe Learning Manager.

## Troubleshooting

**You can't find the connector**

Ask your administrator to enable the connector for you.

**The AI client doesn't remember your earlier question**

Each request is treated as new. Whether a conversation continues depends on your AI client. Repeat the details in your next question.

**The AI client doesn't call the Adobe Learning Manager connector**

Name what you want in your question, such as your enrollments or a course topic.
