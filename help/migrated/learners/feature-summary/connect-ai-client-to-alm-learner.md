# Connect your AI client to Adobe Learning Manager

## Overview

Your integration administrator must configure the connector first. If you don't see an Adobe Learning Manager connector, contact your administrator. **The connector is available only to Adobe Learning Manager Ultimate tier accounts**. Administrators can view [Add Adobe Learning Manager to your enterprise AI client](/help/migrated/administrators/feature-summary/add-alm-to-ai-client.md).

1. Open your organization's AI client, such as Claude or ChatGPT Enterprise.
2. Find the Adobe Learning Manager connector in the list of connectors.
3. Select **Connect**.
4. Authenticate the connection and provide your Adobe Learning Manager account details when prompted.
5. Ask a question about your learning. For ideas, learn more about [sample prompts for learners](/help/migrated/learners/feature-summary/sample-prompts-for-learners.md).

>[!NOTE]
>
>You authenticate once. Your requests run with your own permissions, so you see only what you can access in Adobe Learning Manager.

## Troubleshooting

**You can't find the connector**
Ask your administrator to enable the connector for you.

**The AI client doesn't remember your earlier question**
The server treats each request as new. Whether a conversation continues depends on your AI client. Restate the relevant details in your next question.

**The AI client doesn't call the Adobe Learning Manager connector**
Name what you want in your question, such as your enrollments or a course topic.
