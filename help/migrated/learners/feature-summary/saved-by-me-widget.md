## The Saved by me widget

The **Saved by me** widget displays courses, learning paths, certifications, and job aids that you have bookmarked for later. It gives you a single place to find content you have marked as saved, without needing to search the catalog again.

After an administrator configures the **[!UICONTROL Saved by me]** widget, you can save a learning object (LO) by selecting the ![](assets/saved-by-me-widget5.png) button. You can save an LO on the **[!UICONTROL Home]**, **[!UICONTROL My Learning]**, and **[!UICONTROL Catalog]** pages, and saved LOs appear under the **[!UICONTROL Saved by me]** category on the **[!UICONTROL Home]** page.

You can unsave an LO by selecting the same ![](assets/saved-by-me-widget5.png) button.
