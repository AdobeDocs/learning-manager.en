# Adaptive courses for learners 

## Introduction

Adaptive courses in Adobe Learning Manager are courses that are mandated for you by your author by gauging your learning requirements and customizing them according to your learning requirements once this feature is enabled by your admin. For more information, see [Adaptive Courses](/help/migrated/administrators/feature-summary/adaptive-course-admin.md).

## Take an adaptive course as a learner

When you take an adaptive course in Adobe Learning Manager, you see a personalized set of modules based on the user groups you belong to.

### What you see when you open an adaptive course

When you open an adaptive course, you see only the modules that are relevant to you. Modules assigned to other user groups are not visible to you at all.

Your completion progress bar and the "X of Y modules completed" count reflect only your required modules. Your colleague enrolled in the same course may see a different number of modules, a different mix of required and optional, or an entirely different set of content, all based on their user groups.

### Enroll in an adaptive course

Enrolling in an adaptive course works the same way as enrolling in any course. You can self-enroll from the catalog, or your administrator may enroll you.

![](assets/image_0010.png)

If you have no visible modules in the course based on your current user groups, you cannot enroll. If this happens, contact your administrator. It likely means your user group assignment needs to be updated.

### Navigate modules in the player

When you open the course player, the table of contents on the left shows only the modules you can see. You can use the navigation buttons to move through your modules in sequence (for ordered courses) or access any module in any order (for unordered courses).

Modules that belong to other learners' user groups are not shown in your table of contents.

### Understand your completion requirement

Your course is complete when you have finished all modules labeled **Required**. Optional modules do not block your completion. You can finish them, revisit them, or skip them entirely without affecting your progress toward completion.

### What happens if a classroom or virtual classroom session is full

If a classroom or virtual classroom module is visible to you but has no remaining seats, you are enrolled in the course and placed on a waitlist for that specific session. You can still access and complete all other modules immediately. Only the full session is on hold.

While you are waitlisted on a session:

* The session appears in your course player but you cannot launch or access it.
* Your overall course progress continues normally through your other modules.
* You receive a notification when a seat becomes available and you are moved off the waitlist.

Once a seat is allocated to you, the session becomes accessible, and you can join or complete it.

### What happens when your profile changes mid-course

Your adaptive course experience is tied to your user groups. If your user groups change while you are enrolled, for example, because your role or location changes, your module updates automatically.

* If your updated user groups make previously hidden modules visible to you, those modules appear in your course. If they are required for your new profile, they are added to your completion requirements.
* If your updated user groups no longer include a module, it is removed from your view. If that module was previously required but is no longer, it is removed from your completion count.
* Any module you have already finished remains marked as complete, even if your user groups change. You do not need to redo completed work unless a newly visible module was not previously done.
* Your start date, completion dates for finished modules, and overall progress record stay intact across profile changes.

### What happens after you complete the course

When you complete all your required modules, the course is marked complete. You receive any applicable badge or gamification points at that point.

After completion, if your user groups change and new required modules become available to you, your completion may be rolled back and you may need to complete those additional modules.

When this happens:

* Your original completion remains in your transcript as a historical record.
* The course re-opens in an in-progress state showing only the new required work.
* You only need to complete the newly required modules.
* You receive a notification when a refresh of your completion is triggered.
